# Kotak Mahindra Bank — OD Policy

## OD Policy Update

### OD Tenure

- OD facility allowed for **7 years**.
- Structure:
  - 2 Years Fixed
  - 5 Years Dropline

## Segment 1

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Net Take-Home | ≥ ₹1.5 Lakhs |
| Live Home Loan | > ₹10 Lakhs |
| Product | Fresh & External Balance Transfer |
| Variable Income | Excluded |

### Segment 1 Eligibility

- NTH ≥ ₹1.5 Lakhs.
- Variable income is excluded from NTH calculation.
- Live home loan should be greater than ₹10 Lakhs.
- Applicable only for:
  - Fresh cases
  - External Balance Transfer cases

## Segment 2

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Net Take-Home | ≥ ₹2 Lakhs |
| Scorecard | Green Scorecard |
| Product | Fresh & External Balance Transfer |
| Variable Income | Excluded |

### Segment 2 Eligibility

- NTH ≥ ₹2 Lakhs.
- Variable income is excluded from NTH calculation.
- Green scorecard required.
- Applicable only for:
  - Fresh cases
  - External Balance Transfer cases
