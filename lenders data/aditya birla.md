## Aditya Birla Finance

**Type:** NBFC
**Headline Rate:** 10.99%

### General Criteria

- **Loan Amount:** ₹1L – ₹65L
- **Tenure (PL/OD):** 12–84 months
- **Hybrid OD Tenure:** 1+5, 2+5, 1+6, 2+6, 3+5 years
- **Max FOIR:** Up to 75%
- **Salary Tier 1:** ₹40,000 NTH
- **Salary Tier 2:** ₹35,000 NTH
- **Salary Tier 3:** ₹25,000 NTH
- **Salary Tier 4:** ₹20,000 NTH
- **Minimum Age:** 21 years
- **Maximum Age:** 60 years at maturity
- **Minimum CIBIL:** Scorecard based
- **CIBIL -1:** Not accepted
- **Process:** 100% Digital

### High-Value Loan Criteria

**For ₹65L**
- Company Category A
- NTH ₹2.5L+
- Risk Segment: VLR/LR

**For ₹50L**
- Company Category A-
- NTH ₹1.75L+
- Risk Segment: VLR/LR
