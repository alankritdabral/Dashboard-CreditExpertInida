---

## 1. WFH Documentation Relaxation

### Existing Norm

The following WFH documentation was required:

- HR Mail Confirmation
- Supervisor Mail Confirmation
- HR Communication regarding WFH

### Current Change

- WFH declaration is **not required**
- WFH documentation is **not required**
- Positive Employment Verification is **mandatory**
- No change in co-applicant norms
- All other norms remain unchanged

---

## 2. Balance Transfer Documentation Simplification

### Existing Norm

For BT loans:

- 6 months banking / SOA required

For Credit Card BT:

- 3 months credit card statement required

### Current Change

- Only **3 months banking statement** required for all cases:
  - BT
  - Non-BT
- BT cases must be tracked for **3 months positive EMI clearance**
- Credit Card BT requires only **1 month statement**

### Key Checks

- No late-fee charges
- No over-limit charges
- Existing deviation norms remain unchanged

---

## 3. RTGS Option for BT Deals

### Existing Norm

- Fund release through DD only

### Current Change

RTGS has been introduced as an additional fund-release option alongside DD.

### Mandatory Conditions

- RTGS details must be available in FCL
- Loan closure proof must be collected as PDD
- Ensure funds are not refunded to the customer's account by the financier

---

## 4. Benefits of the Policy Change

The policy update highlights:

- Faster processing
- Reduced documentation
- Better customer experience
- Improved operational efficiency
- Greater flexibility in BT processing

---

## 5. Effective Immediately

The creative states that the following changes are effective immediately:

- Simpler documentation
- Faster decisions
- Better customer experience

### Credit Policy

All other underwriting and credit policy norms remain unchanged.
