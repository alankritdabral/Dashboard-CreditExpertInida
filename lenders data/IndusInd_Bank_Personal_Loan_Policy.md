# 8. Personal Loan Pre-Login Checklist — June 26

## Income & Banking Documents

### Salary Documents

- Last consecutive **3 months Salary Slips / Salary Certificate**
- Latest **3 months bank statements with salary credits**
- Salary credits in the form of **IMPS, UPI and Cheque** are not allowed.
- Gap should not be more than **7 days from CPA maker movement date**.

---

# 9. Employment Stability

Any of the following documents can be accepted to prove employment stability for salaried applicants:

1. **Form 16**
2. **Date of joining mentioned on Salary Slip**
3. **Appointment Letter / Increment Letter**
4. **Certificate / letter from employer clearly mentioning date of joining**

### Employment Stability Requirements

**1 year current employer stability proof** or **2 years overall stability proof** is required for:

- **Cat C/C1000**
- **Cat A/B/G**, if age is less than 28 years or 3 salary credits from current employer are not available.

# 11. Permanent Address Proof & Contact Details

### Permanent Address Proof

Permanent address proof is a **mandatory requirement**.

The following documents are acceptable as proof of permanent address:

- Registered Sale Agreement
- Share Certificate issued by Housing Society
- Property Tax Receipts
- Utility Bills — maximum 1 year old:
  - Electricity bill
  - Water bill
  - Gas bill
- Society Maintenance Bill
- Passport
- Voter ID
- Driving License
- UID / Aadhaar Card

For certain cases, the following may also be used with the **name and address clearly mentioned**:

- Father
- Mother
- Spouse
- Brother
- Sister
- Father-in-law
- Mother-in-law

### When Permanent Address Proof Is Required

Permanent Address (PA) proof is required where the current residence is rented for:

- NCT customers
- Bachelor accommodation cases
- Employees of CAT C/C1000 Companies
- Employees of CAT A/B/G where current residence stability is **<1 year** and NMI is **< ₹50,000**

# 13. Documents Required for Balance Transfer Application

### BT Requirements

- Maximum **5 BT loans permitted**
- BT as per **Approved List of Financiers only**
- At least **one of the BT loans should have cleared at least 3 EMIs**
- Any one of the following documents of BT loan:
  - Repayment Schedule
  - Sanction Letter
  - SOA
- Details should mention:
  - Name of Bank / Financier
  - Sanction date
  - EMI amount
- **3 months EMI clearance bank statement**

# 16. Exception Approval to Login

> Any exception to the login may be approved by the **RCM with RSM concurrence**.

Such approvals will be treated strictly as **login exceptions only**. The final credit decision will be based on a **detailed appraisal of the application**.
