# Piramal Finance — Personal Loan Policy

## Personal Loan Highlights

| Parameter | Policy |
|---|---|
| Loan Amount | ₹1 Lakh to ₹30 Lakhs |
| Minimum Net Salary | ₹25,000/month |
| Minimum Age | 21 Years |
| Maximum Age at Maturity | 60 Years |
| Tenure | 12 to 72 Months |
| FOIR | Up to 75% |
| Starting ROI | 11.99% |
| External Balance Transfer | Allowed up to 3 PLs |
| Work From Home | Accepted |

## Eligibility Criteria

### Employment / Profile

- V15+ and above profiles mentioned.
- Unlisted / open-market cases can also be sourced.
- Work From Home profiles accepted.
- Government Grade 4 employees can be accepted up to ₹5 Lakhs.
- -1 & 0 CIBIL cases may be accepted subject to policy.
- Recent funding / fresh loan cases accepted.

## Income Eligibility

- Minimum net salary: ₹25,000/month.
- Applicable to private and government categories as mentioned in the policy.

## Loan Amount

- Minimum loan amount: ₹1 Lakh.
- Maximum loan amount: ₹30 Lakhs.

## CIBIL

- Standard profile requirement mentioned: CIBIL 750+.
- -1 and 0 CIBIL cases may also be accepted subject to policy.

## Balance Transfer

- External Balance Transfer allowed.
- Maximum 3 personal loans can be considered for BT.
- Recent / live loans may be accepted subject to the stated policy.
- Any live loan with last 6 months pending EMI is not obligated as per the one-pager.

## FOIR

- FOIR eligibility up to 75%.

## Gold Loan

- Gold loan obligation is not required as stated in the policy.

## Employment Experience

- Minimum 6 months total experience required.
- Multiple organisations can be considered for experience calculation.

## Work From Home

- WFH profiles are accepted.

## ROI

| Parameter | Policy |
|---|---|
| Starting ROI | 11.99% |
| Maximum ROI Mentioned | 27.49% |

## Tenure

- Minimum tenure: 12 Months
- Maximum tenure: 72 Months

## Other Highlights

- 100% digital process from login to disbursement.
- No locking period mentioned.
- Zero partial payment / foreclosure charges as stated in the one-pager.
- Fast disbursement.
- Flexible tenure.
- Recent funding can be accepted.
- Unlisted / open-market cases can be sourced.
- Government Grade 4 employees accepted up to ₹5 Lakhs.
- WFH profiles accepted.

> **Note:** Final eligibility, pricing and approval are subject to Piramal Finance credit policy.
