# Lender Eligibility Policies

This document outlines the detailed eligibility criteria and policies for all banks and NBFCs configured in the system. The rules include general parameters, balance transfer (BT) guidelines, obligation calculations, income add-backs, and specific profile restrictions.

---

## 1. Axis Bank

**Type:** Private Bank | **Headline Rate:** 9.99%

### General Criteria

- **Loan Amount:** ₹1,00,000 - ₹40,00,000
- **Tenure:** Up to 84 months
- **Max FOIR:** 70% (increased only if user has homeloan)
- **Age Requirement:** 21 to 60 years at maturity
- **Minimum Salary:** ₹35,000
- **Minimum CIBIL:** 700
- **CIBIL -1 (New to Credit):** Not Accepted

### Balance Transfer (BT) Rules

- **Personal Loan BT:** Allowed (Multiple PLs allowed, Unlimited)
- **Credit Card BT:** Allowed (Unlimited)
- **App Loan BT:** Not Allowed
- **Overdraft BT:** Not Allowed
- **Top-Up:** Allowed

### Obligations & Add-backs

- **Obligations:** CC Outstanding (4%), Gold Loan (0%). PLs with < 6 EMIs remaining and HLs with < 12 EMIs remaining are considered non-obligations.
- **Income Add-backs:** Yearly- bonus(lowest of last 2 years)/24*75%,
  Half Yearly- (yearly bonus)/2%
  Quarterly- Lowest of last 2 quarter/6*75%.

### Restrictions & Special Conditions

- **Restricted Profiles:** Government School Teacher, Class IV Employee, Local Municipality Staff, Defence & Army, State Government Employee.
- **Other:** No WFH accepted, no own-house mandate, no specific geo-limits.

### Special Offers (Valid till 30th Sep'26)

_Note: GST is applicable on processing fees (PF) up to a max of 2% + GST. System capability is WIP; log cases via CDS in the interim. For offer related queries, please contact your PL Sales Manager._

- **SCAT A / CAT A / STF:** 9.99% for ≥₹15L (PF ₹6,999/-) | 10.35% for ≥₹10L (PF ₹4,999/-)
- **CAT B / Govt:** 10.39% for ≥₹15L (PF ₹6,999/-) | 10.45% for ≥₹10L (PF ₹4,999/-)
- **CAT C:** 10.59% for ≥₹15L (PF ₹6,999/-) | 10.75% for ≥₹10L (PF ₹4,999/-)

**2. Top-Up Loan Offer (Relaxed ROI)**

- **CIBIL ≥750:** Same as base ROI (minimum 10.49%)
- **CIBIL <750:** 50 bps higher than base ROI
- _Criteria:_ Applicable across SCAT A, CAT A, Staff, CAT B, CAT C, EDU, MED, Govt. Pricing via CDS initiation.

**3. Exclusive Government Employee Offer**

- **ROI:** 10.90% | **PF:** ₹4,999/-
- _Criteria:_ Loan ≥₹5L, CIBIL 750+, Income ≥₹50,000. Fresh & WIP cases eligible (No internal BT).

**4. Special Pricing – Select Corporates (Axis Salary Acc Only)**

**Type:** NBFC | **Headline Rate:** 10.99%

- **Tenure (PL/OD):** 12 - 84 months

- **Minimum CIBIL:** Scorecard based

- **Process:** 100% Digital, Same-day Login to Disbursal

### High-Value Loan Criteria

- **For ₹65L:** Company Category A, NTH ₹2.5L+, Risk Segment: VLR/LR
- **For ₹50L:** Company Category A-, NTH ₹1.75L+, Risk Segment: VLR/LR

### Employee Funding

- **CAT A:** ₹65L (T&C apply)
- **CAT B:** ₹40L
- **CAT C:** ₹30L / ₹35L / ₹40L (scorecard dependent)
- **CAT D:** ₹10L / ₹15L / ₹25L / ₹30L (scorecard dependent)
- **Proprietor / Partnership Employees:** ₹5L / ₹8L (scorecard dependent)

### Products

- Personal Loan
- Hybrid Term Loan / Hybrid OD
- Balance Transfer – PL & CC
- Paperless BT
- Drop-line OD
- OD to OD BT

### Balance Transfer (BT) Rules

- **Personal Loan BT:** Allowed (Max 6 combined PL/CC BTs)
- **Credit Card BT:** Allowed (Max 5 CC-only BTs)
- **App Loan BT:** Not Allowed
- **OD BT:** OD to OD
- **Top-Up:** Allowed
- **3–5 MOB PL BT – Allowed Lenders:** HDFC, SBI, Axis, Kotak, ICICI, Bajaj, Tata Capital (only where BT loan amount > ₹5L)

### Obligations & Add-backs

- **Obligations:** CC Outstanding (5%), Gold Loan (0%). Loans with <6 EMIs must be de-obligated.
- **Income Add-backs:** Rental Income (50%), Monthly/Quarterly/Half-Yearly Incentive, Monthly/Quarterly Bonus, Yearly Bonus (considered for VLR/LR/MR), LTA, Monthly Variable.

### Documentation & FI Flexibility

- No sanction condition / No Post-Sanction Conditions
- Salary slip not required if Official Mail ID verified
- Office FI waived with EPFO + OMID + HRMS + 26AS
- Residence FI waiver subject to specified HL/LAP + CIBIL conditions

- **General Policy:** Pan India Sourcing, WFH Accepted, Own House Property (OHP) Not Mandatory. Field Investigation (FI) waived for loans below ₹7.50 Lakhs.
